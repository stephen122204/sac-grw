# Status of the research archive

The round-numbered research files record development, including rejected methods,
incorrect diagnostics, and later repairs. They are retained for provenance, not
presented as independently validated methods or interchangeable evidence.

- Compensation, velocity-set, finite-N attribution, MLMC, and conservative-signed
  experiments are historical branches, not the current manuscript's contribution.
- Round 5/6 sensitivity claims and round 10 differential-obstruction claims were
  superseded. Use the explicit round-7 counterexample and manuscript proof.
- Round 13 used process-salted hashes; exact replay was unavailable. It is not the
  selected-plan evidence in this paper. Round 17 and round 23 use stable keys.
- The round-23 separation diagnostic evolves the wrong comparator. Only its
  variance comparisons are used; round 24 supplies the repaired geometry.
- Separated signs do not imply pathwise equality. Round 25 provides the repeated
  divergence check. Only the one-sign condition gives the stated unconditional
  equality guarantee.
- Historical `guaranteed_share` fields denote an estimated final-only reduction
  divided by an estimated full-history reduction. They are not a lower bound on
  a fraction of terminal gain.
- The shared coupling core replaces the round-6 function without changing its
  returned trajectories: 24 cells spanning both nonlinear fluxes, all four
  policies, and three particle counts match the saved pre-extraction function
  exactly. Historical recorded hashes refer to their original versions.

See `manuscript/REVISION-AUDIT.md` and the article evidence table for current
claims. Old text, console output, and model-generated interpretations are not
additional scientific evidence.
